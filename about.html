Sources & attribution · आधाराः

This site is built on top of data obtained from the following sources. We are deeply grateful to them.

advaitasharada.sringeri.net

Dakshinamnaya Sri Sharada Peetham, Sringeri — digital texts of the Śaṅkara-bhāṣyas, Ānandagiri's ṭīkās, Sureśvara's vārttikas and other commentaries hosted here.

Mahesh Research Institute — Dakshinamurti Math Publications

The printed Upaniṣadbhāṣyam is our base edition: the reference against which typos in the digital texts are corrected, and the source of the printed pratīka marks.

Their Prakaraṇadvādaśī (ed. S. Subrahmanya Shastri, 2nd edition, 2004; a free download from the Mahesh Research Institute) is the source of Padmapāda's Vedāntasāra ṭīkā on the Ātmabodha shown here.

Sri Tamal Maharaj — RKMVERI

The word-level digital reader of the Prasthānatrayī with Śaṅkara's bhāṣya (deposit, CC BY 4.0; paper arXiv:2607.07282): the bhāṣya word analysis behind the word cards. His Vedānta reader is the source of the padaccheda and word glosses of Ānandagiri's ṭīkās, and the padaccheda of Sureśvara's vārttikas and the Vaiyāsika-nyāya-mālā, shown here (where not our own). His word-by-word Gītā readers with Śrīdhara's Subodhinī and Madhusūdana's Gūḍhārthadīpikā are the source of those two Gītā commentaries here — his corrected text, with what the reading edition dropped restored from GRETIL or the printed edition — and of their word glosses. His Śaṅkara reader is the source of Acyutakṛṣṇānanda Tīrtha's Vanamālā on the Taittirīya-bhāṣya here — its text, its alignment to the bhāṣya, and its word glosses; its pratīkas are his marks together with the bold of the Mahesh Research Institute print. The same reader is the source of Govindānanda's Ratnaprabhā on the Brahmasūtra-bhāṣya and of Pūrṇānanda Sarasvatī's Pūrṇānandī on the Ratnaprabhā (shown as fold-outs under the Ratnaprabhā notes they explain), with their alignment and word glosses; pāṭhāntaras his e-text records are marked ⚑.

Gita Supersite — IIT Kanpur

The digital texts of Nīlakaṇṭha's Bhāvadīpa and of Dhanapati Sūri's Bhāṣyotkarṣadīpikā on the Gītā (and the base of Sri Tamal Maharaj's Śrīdhara and Madhusūdana texts). Suspected misprints in the e-text are shown as printed and marked ⚑.

Internet Archive — eGangotri

The scan of the Nirṇayasāgara Press edition (1916) of Śaṅkarānanda's Gītātātparyabodhinī, from the collection of the Kashmiri Sanskrit scholar B. N. Kalla: the base of our Śaṅkarānandī text, which we read from the scan. The Bhole Baba edition (scan) was used to check it.

Internet Archive — MPL Sastry Library / indoscripts.org (ISRT)

The scan of the Trivandrum Sanskrit Series edition (no. 7, T. Ganapati Sastri, 1909) of Sadāśivendra Sarasvatī's Brahmatattvaprakāśikā, with its two manuscripts' readings (क/ख): the base of our Brahmatattvaprakāśikā text, which we read from the scan. The Sri Vani Vilas Press, Srirangam edition (scan) was used to check it. Our corrections of the print's misprints are marked ⚑ with the printed form.
