Sources & attribution · आधाराः

This site is built on top of data obtained from the following sources. We are deeply grateful to them.

advaitasharada.sringeri.net

Dakshinamnaya Sri Sharada Peetham, Sringeri — digital texts of the Śaṅkara-bhāṣyas, Ānandagiri's ṭīkās, Sureśvara's vārttikas and other commentaries hosted here.

Mahesh Research Institute — Dakshinamurti Math Publications

The printed Upaniṣadbhāṣyam is our base edition: the reference against which typos in the digital texts are corrected, and the source of the printed pratīka marks.

Sri Tamal Maharaj — RKMVERI

The word-level digital reader of the Prasthānatrayī with Śaṅkara's bhāṣya (deposit, CC BY 4.0; paper arXiv:2607.07282): the bhāṣya word analysis behind the word cards. His Vedānta reader is the source of the padaccheda and word glosses of Ānandagiri's ṭīkās, and the padaccheda of Sureśvara's vārttikas and the Vaiyāsika-nyāya-mālā, shown here (where not our own).

upasanayoga.org — Sri Aruna-ji

A. K. Aruna's editions, used for the bhāṣya's mūla-pratīka marks.
