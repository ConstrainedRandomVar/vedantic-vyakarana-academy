⚙ Settings — what each option does

Open ⚙ Settings from the homepage or from the bar at the top of any reading page; it is the same panel in both places. Every choice is saved in this browser and applies to every page.

Text size

Makes all text on the reading pages larger or smaller (85% to 120%): the mūla, the commentaries, headings and word cards.

Script

Shows the Sanskrit in Devanāgarī, IAST, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati and other scripts. The text itself does not change, only the letters used to write it.

Word grammar on hover

Every word of the mūla, and of most commentaries, has a grammar card: its meaning, its प्रकृति (dhātu or prātipadika), vibhakti or लकार, kāraka role, and how a compound breaks up. Turn this on to see a word's card whenever the mouse rests on it. When it is off (the default), hold Cmd/Ctrl or Alt while pointing to see one. On phones and tablets, tap a word.

Commentary markers

Small coloured tags in the bhāṣya that show how an argument works. All are off by default, for a clean reading text; turn on the ones you want.

Source of proof (प्रमाण) tags a statement as resting on scripture (श्रुतिः), reasoning (युक्तिः) or experience (अनुभवः).
Concession (अभ्यु॰) marks where the commentator grants a point for argument's sake before answering it.
महावाक्यम् flags an identity statement such as तत्त्वमसि or अहं ब्रह्मास्मि.

Commentary fold-outs

Some commentary layers (for example a ṭīkā or the vārttika's sub-commentary) are folded away by default so the page stays readable. Choose whether each opens collapsed or expanded when a page loads. The टीका ⇕ button on a page opens or closes them on that page only.

Page buttons

⚑ Report an issue (shown by default) lets you tell us about a wrong word, gloss or analysis, on reading pages and in the Sanskrit gym. Turn it off if you don't need it.
⇉ Share reading (hidden by default) is for a teacher leading a class: you get a link to send to students, and their pages follow your scrolling live, each reader in their own script.

In the grammar practice (संस्कृत-भाषा-व्यायाम-शाला), moves to the next question by itself after you answer. Turn it off to stay on the answer and read the explanation first.
