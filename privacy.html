Privacy

अध्ययन-सहकारी is a free reading aid for Vedānta texts. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not run a server that receives your data. Nothing you save leaves your browser.

What stays in your browser

Your bookmarks, collections, highlights (with their notes and tags), study notes, recent searches and settings are saved in your own browser's storage on this device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes them, so use My library → Backup → Export to keep a copy.

When you send a report

A ⚑ report (on a word card, on a practice question, or the “report an issue” button on a reading page) is sent only when you press Send. It carries what you write, the name and email you choose to give (both optional; your browser remembers them for next time), the word or question with what the page showed for it, the page link, and your device type, browser and screen size (a reading-page report also includes the verse in view and your browser’s user-agent string, all shown to you before you send). Word-card and reading-page reports go through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and practice-question reports through Formspree (formspree.io). Both are form-to-email services that pass the report on to us by email. If the service cannot be reached, a word-card or reading-page report opens your mail app with the same text, and a practice-question report offers a pre-filled GitHub issue instead.

Links to other sites

Some buttons open other websites in a new tab, for example the 📖 dictionary (sanskritkosha.com), the full-text search, or source libraries. Those sites have their own privacy practices; nothing from your library is sent to them.
