3.1.1
तदन्तरप्रतिपत्तौ रंहति सम्परिष्वक्तः प्रश्ननिरूपणाभ्याम्
on obtaining another body (देहान्तरप्रतिपत्तौ); moves, goes (= गच्छति, to another body); enveloped, embraced (by subtle elements, body-seeds); because of the question and the answer (निरूपण)
3.1.2
त्र्यात्मकत्वात्तु भूयस्त्वात्
because of being three-natured (tejas-ap-anna); but/rather; because of preponderance (of water/liquid in the body)
3.1.3
प्राणगतेश्च
and because of the going of the prāṇas
3.1.4
अग्न्यादिगतिश्रुतेरिति चेन्न भाक्तत्वात्
because scripture states (the prāṇas') going to Agni etc.; if (so), (we say) no; because (that statement) is figurative (gauṇī)
3.1.5
प्रथमेऽश्रवणादिति चेन्न ता एव ह्युपपत्तेः
"because [water] is not heard in the first [fire]" — thus; if [so urged], no — not [a fault]; those very [waters] (= āpaḥ, feminine); only — those very [waters]; for, because of [its] fitness (upapatti)
3.1.6
अश्रुतत्वादिति चेन्नेष्टादिकारिणां प्रतीतेः
"because of non-mention (in śruti)" — thus (the objection); if so — no; of the iṣṭā-etc.-performers; because of (their) being cognized (recognized in the passage)
3.1.7
भाक्तं वानात्मवित्त्वात्तथाहि दर्शयति
secondary, figurative (their being 'food'); not literal; or — because (souls) are not knowers of the Self; for thus; (the śruti) shows, declares
3.1.8
कृतात्ययेऽनुशयवान्दृष्टस्मृतिभ्यां यथेतमनेवं च
When fructified karma is exhausted, [soul] still has residue; by śruti and smṛti; By the same path it came, and [yet] not exactly so; and
3.1.9
चरणादिति चेन्नोपलक्षणार्थेति कार्ष्णाजिनिः
"from conduct" — thus (the objection runs); "if so — no; (the śruti) serves to indicate the anuśaya"; so thinks the teacher Kārṣṇājini
3.1.10
आनर्थक्यमिति चेन्न तदपेक्षत्वात्
"purposelessness" (of conduct would result) — thus the objection; if so — no (this is no fault); because (ritual karma) depends on that (conduct)
3.1.11
सुकृतदुष्कृते एवेति तु बादरिः
good and bad deeds alone (the two); "only/alone" — thus; but (marks Bādari's view); Bādari (the teacher) holds
3.1.12
अनिष्टादिकारिणामपि च श्रुतम्
of those too who do undesired acts etc.; and, also; [it is] heard/declared in śruti
3.1.13
संयमने त्वनुभूयेतरेषामारोहावरोहौ तद्गतिदर्शनात्
in Saṃyamana (Yama's abode, the place of restraint); but the others, having suffered (Yama's torments), ascend and descend; because scripture shows their subjection (going to Yama)
3.1.14
स्मरन्ति च
(the cultured/śiṣṭāḥ) teach it in smṛti too; and; moreover (api ca)
3.1.15
अपि च सप्त
moreover; and also; and (conjunctive); seven (the seven hells)
3.1.16
तत्रापि च तद्व्यापारादविरोधः
there too (even in those seven hells); and; no contradiction, since His (Yama's) controlling function operates
3.1.17
विद्याकर्मणोरिति तु प्रकृतत्वात्
"of vidyā and karman" (bhāṣya: = 'of these two paths'); but, rather (marks the settled conclusion); because it is the topic under discussion
3.1.18
न तृतीये तथोपलब्धेः
not; in the third (station); because it is so observed/perceived
3.1.19
स्मर्यतेऽपि च लोके
is also recorded/remembered (in smṛti tradition); and; in the world (common usage/experience)
3.1.20
दर्शनाच्च
and because of (the) observation (of origination)
3.1.21
तृतीयशब्दावरोधः संशोकजस्य
inclusion (of sweat-born) within the third term 'udbhijja'; of the moisture/sweat-born creature (its inclusion explained)
3.1.22
साभाव्यापत्तिरुपपत्तेः
Attainment of same nature (as ether etc.), because (only that) is tenable.
3.1.23
नातिचिरेण विशेषात्
not after a very long time (they descend); because of the specifying scriptural statement (viśeṣa-darśana)
3.1.24
अन्याधिष्ठितेषु पूर्ववदभिलापात्
in those (rice-plants) presided over by other souls; as before, because of the scriptural wording
3.1.25
अशुद्धमिति चेन्न शब्दात्
"(the sacrifice is) impure" — thus (the objection); if (so), then no (it is not); because of scripture (the Veda's word)
3.1.26
रेतःसिग्योगोऽथ
and connection with the semen-effuser (the father)
3.1.27
योनेः शरीरम्
from the womb (birth-source); the body is born
3.2.1
सन्ध्ये सृष्टिराह हि
in the saṃdhya (dream-state, junction of waking and deep-sleep); creation is — (the śruti) declares it (so); for, because (indeed)
3.2.2
निर्मातारं चैके पुत्रादयश्च
the maker (of desires), i.e. the Self — object of 'they teach'; and some (śākhins) teach (it); and sons etc. (are intended there as the 'desires')
3.2.3
मायामात्रं तु कार्त्स्न्येनानभिव्यक्तस्वरूपत्वात्
mere illusion; nothing but māyā; but (excludes the prior pāramārthika view); because its nature is not fully manifested (as real)
3.2.4
सूचकश्च हि श्रुतेराचक्षते च तद्विदः
(the dream) is also indicative/a foreteller, and; for, indeed (giving the reason); from scripture (it is known); and they declare; and, also; the knowers of it (dream-interpreters)
3.2.5
पराभिध्यानात्तु तिरोहितं ततो ह्यस्य बन्धविपर्ययौ
but through meditation on the Supreme Lord; (it is) hidden, concealed by nescience; from that, i.e. the Lord as cause; for, of this (jīva); bondage and its reverse, i.e. liberation
3.2.6
देहयोगाद्वा सोऽपि
or because of connection with the body; that (concealment of knowledge-lordship) too
3.2.7
तदभावो नाडीषु तच्छ्रुतेरात्मनि च
the absence of that (of the dream/dream-vision); in the nāḍīs (channels); because of scripture on that, and in the Self; and (also; in the Self as well)
3.2.8
अतः प्रबोधोऽस्मात्
therefore, for this reason; awakening (arises) from this (the Self)
3.2.9
स एव तु कर्मानुस्मृतिशब्दविधिभ्यः
that very same (jīva); the very same, precisely; but (marking the settled conclusion); because of work, recollection, scripture, and injunction
3.2.10
मुग्धेऽर्धसम्पत्तिः परिशेषात्
In the swooned one, (there is) half-attainment (of deep sleep); by elimination / from what remains over
3.2.11
न स्थानतोऽपि परस्योभयलिङ्गं सर्वत्र हि
not; not even by location (adjunct-connection); of the supreme (Brahman); dual-charactered; everywhere (in all śruti-passages); for, because
3.2.12
न भेदादिति चेन्न प्रत्येकमतद्वचनात्
not; the siddhāntin's refuting 'no' (neti brūmaḥ); 'because of difference (in forms)' — thus the objector; if (so urged), then no; because in each case there is no statement of that (difference)
3.2.13
अपि चैवमेके
also, moreover (emphatic/connective particle); and thus some (śākhins/schools) too teach
3.2.14
अरूपवदेव हि तत्प्रधानत्वात्
as the formless indeed (Brahman is to be known without form); for, because (giving the reason); because the formless is their chief import
3.2.15
प्रकाशवच्चावैयर्थ्यात्
And (Brahman is) like light; because (the texts') non-purposelessness
3.2.16
आह च तन्मात्रम्
(the śruti) says, declares; and; consisting of That (consciousness) alone
3.2.17
दर्शयति चाथो अपि स्मर्यते
(the śruti) shows/reveals Brahman by negation; and then / and moreover; also, too (in smṛti as well); it is remembered / handed down in smṛti
3.2.18
अत एव चोपमा सूर्यकादिवत्
for this very reason; hence; verily, precisely (emphatic particle); and the simile (is employed); like the sun's reflection (in water) and the like
3.2.19
अम्बुवदग्रहणात्तु न तथात्वम्
but because (the Self) is not apprehended like water; not; such likeness (to the water-sun example holds)
3.2.20
वृद्धिह्रासभाक्त्वमन्तर्भावादुभयसामञ्जस्यादेवम्
Partaking of growth-decay (in Brahman) arises from inclusion in upādhis; thus both (analogy, subject) accord
3.2.21
दर्शनाच्च
and because scripture shows (Brahman's entry into upādhis)
3.2.22
प्रकृतैतावत्त्वं हि प्रतिषेधति ततो ब्रवीति च भूयः
the being-limited-to-just-this-much of what is under discussion; for, indeed (giving the reason); (the negation 'neti neti') denies, negates; thereafter, then, after that; (the text/śruti) declares, speaks; and; further, again, something more (besides)
3.2.23
तदव्यक्तमाह हि
(śruti) declares that (Brahman to be) unmanifest; for, because (gives the reason)
3.2.24
अपि च संराधने प्रत्यक्षानुमानाभ्याम्
also, moreover; and; at the time of devout meditative worship; by perception and inference, i.e. Śruti and Smṛti
3.2.25
प्रकाशादिवच्चावैशेष्यं प्रकाशश्च कर्मण्यभ्यासात्
And non-distinction, as with light etc.; And light (the example) appears so by upādhi; In the adjunct-act; from repeated teaching (in Vedānta)
3.2.26
अतोऽनन्तेन तथा हि लिङ्गम्
hence, with the infinite (supreme Self); for so it is, thus indeed; for, because; the indicatory mark (of śruti) exists
3.2.27
उभयव्यपदेशात्त्वहिकुण्डलवत्
But, from the twofold designation (of difference and non-difference), like snake and its coil
3.2.28
प्रकाशाश्रयवद्वा तेजस्त्वात्
or, like light and its locus; because of both being of the nature of light
3.2.29
पूर्ववद्वा
As before — the prior analogy (प्रकाशादिवत्, non-difference) is the conclusion
3.2.30
प्रतिषेधाच्च
and because of the (scriptural) prohibition (of any other)
3.2.31
परमतः सेतून्मानसम्बन्धभेदव्यपदेशेभ्यः
beyond this (Brahman, something other) exists; from designations of bridge, measure, connection, difference
3.2.32
सामान्यात्तु
But (the setu-term applies) only from a common feature
3.2.33
बुद्ध्यर्थः पादवत्
having cognition as purpose, i.e. for meditation; like the quarters/feet (an analogy)
3.2.34
स्थानविशेषात्प्रकाशादिवत्
From the particular locus (upādhi), as with light (sun/moon) etc.
3.2.35
उपपत्तेश्च
and because (this relation) is logically fitting
3.2.36
तथाऽन्यप्रतिषेधात्
Likewise, because of the negation of any other (than Brahman)
3.2.37
अनेन सर्वगतत्वमायामशब्दादिभ्यः
by this (refutation of the bridge-designation, etc.); all-pervadingness (is known) from the word āyāma (extent), etc.
3.2.38
फलमत उपपत्तेः
the fruit (of action) — from this (the Lord); because (it is) logically established / reasonable
3.2.39
श्रुतत्वाच्च
and because it is scripturally heard (taught)
3.2.40
धर्मं जैमिनिरत एव
dharma (merit), held to be the bestower of the fruit; Jaimini (the teacher); from this, hence; alone, for this very (reason)
3.2.41
पूर्वं तु बादरायणो हेतुव्यपदेशात्
the former (previously-stated) view; but (excludes the other view); Bādarāyaṇa (the teacher, the subject); because [the Lord] is designated as the cause
3.3.1
सर्ववेदान्तप्रत्ययं चोदनाद्यविशेषात्
the cognition is the same in all Vedānta-texts; because of non-difference of injunction (codanā) etc.
3.3.2
भेदान्नेति चेन्नैकस्यामपि
"not [one vidyā], because of difference [in the known]" — so (pūrvapakṣa); if so — no; for even in one [vidyā such difference holds]
3.3.3
स्वाध्यायस्य तथात्वेन हि समाचारेऽधिकाराच्च सववच्च तन्नियमः
of the (Vedic) recitation / one's own study; because of being so (a property of svādhyāya); for, because; in the Samācāra text, and from the (scriptural) context; and like the savas (the seven Saurya sacrifices); that restriction (of the vow to the Atharvaṇikas)
3.3.4
दर्शयति च
(scripture) also shows / declares it; and, also (connective)
3.3.5
उपसंहारोऽर्थाभेदाद्विधिशेषवत्समाने च
subsuming (occurs), since the object is non-different, as with injunction-subsidiaries, in the same (cognition); and
3.3.6
अन्यथात्वं शब्दादिति चेन्नाविशेषात्
otherness, difference (between the two vidyās); because of the (differing) text — thus (the objection); if so, no — because there is no real distinction
3.3.7
न वा प्रकरणभेदात्परोवरीयस्त्वादिवत्
not (i.e. unity of vidyā is not right); or rather (setting aside the prior view); because of context (commencement) difference, as in the paro-varīyas meditation etc.
3.3.8
संज्ञातश्चेत्तदुक्तमस्ति तु तदपि
If (vidyā be one) from the name — that has been answered; but, however; that (oneness of name) too does exist
3.3.9
व्याप्तेश्च समञ्जसम्
and because of (its) pervasiveness/comprehensiveness; (the identity-view is) proper, fitting, consistent
3.3.10
सर्वाभेदादन्यत्रेमे
since prāṇa is non-different everywhere, these qualities apply elsewhere too
3.3.11
आनन्दादयः प्रधानस्य
bliss and the rest (Brahman's attributes); of the principal (qualificand), i.e. Brahman
3.3.12
प्रियशिरस्त्वाद्यप्राप्तिरुपचयापचयौ हि भेदे
non-attainment elsewhere of attributes like pleasure-as-head; and increase-decrease; for, because; when difference exists (locative absolute)
3.3.13
इतरे त्वर्थसामान्यात्
the other dharmas (bliss, etc.); but, from sameness of referent (the one Brahman)
3.3.14
आध्यानाय प्रयोजनाभावात्
for meditation/contemplation (on the supreme Self); because of the absence of any purpose
3.3.15
आत्मशब्दाच्च
and because (the śruti uses) the word 'ātman' for the puruṣa
3.3.16
आत्मगृहीतिरितरवदुत्तरात्
Grasping of the (supreme) Ātman, like elsewhere, from the subsequent text
3.3.17
अन्वयादिति चेत्स्यादवधारणात्
"because of the [sentence's] syntactic connection" — thus [the objector]; if so, [it] could be [Paramātman's grasping], from emphatic determination
3.3.18
कार्याख्यानादपूर्वम्
Since the text states an already-established act, āchamana is not a new injunction
3.3.19
समान एवं चाभेदात्
the same (one and identical vidyā); thus, in this very same way; likewise; and because of (their) non-difference
3.3.20
सम्बन्धादेवमन्यत्रापि
From the [single-vidyā] connection, thus elsewhere too
3.3.21
न वा विशेषात्
not (refuting the prior pūrvapakṣa view); nay (with न, sets aside the prior view); because of the distinction of meditation-locus
3.3.22
दर्शयति च
(scripture) shows, reveals, points out (by an indicatory mark); and; moreover (अपि च)
3.3.23
सम्भृतिद्युव्याप्त्यपि चातः
the glories sambhṛti and heaven-pervasion (vibhūtis) also; and from this (reason, the special abode) too
3.3.24
पुरुषविद्यायामिव चेतरेषामनाम्नानात्
as in the Puruṣavidyā (teaching); and because of non-mention for the others (Taittirīyas)
3.3.25
वेधाद्यर्थभेदात्
because of difference in purpose of mantras etc. beginning with 'piercing'
3.3.26
हानौ तूपायनशब्दशेषत्वात्कुशाच्छन्दस्तुत्युपगानवत्तदुक्तम्
when only the relinquishing (of merit and demerit) is heard; but (upāyana is to be supplied), since the word upāyana is complementary—like kuśa, chandas, stuti, upagāna; this has been stated
3.3.27
साम्पराये तर्तव्याभावात्तथा ह्यन्ये
at the transit/departure from the body (at death); because nothing remains to be crossed/traversed; likewise; for others (Kauṣītakins) too read it thus
3.3.28
छन्दत उभयाविरोधात्
voluntarily, of one's own free will; because of non-contradiction between both (the two Śruti texts)
3.3.29
गतेरर्थवत्त्वमुभयथाऽन्यथा हि विरोधः
the path's having-purpose holds both ways; otherwise; for; indeed; contradiction would arise
3.3.30
उपपन्नस्तल्लक्षणार्थोपलब्धेर्लोकवत्
(This) is tenable, since the purpose marked by that is perceived — as in the world
3.3.31
अनियमः सर्वासामविरोधः शब्दानुमानाभ्याम्
non-restriction (the path applies to all vidyās); no contradiction in this for all the vidyās; by scripture and inference (the two grounds)
3.3.32
यावदधिकारमवस्थितिराधिकारिकाणाम्
Office-holders remain embodied as long as their office lasts
3.3.33
अक्षरधियां त्ववरोधः सामान्यतद्भावाभ्यामौपसदवत्तदुक्तम्
of the cognitions concerning the Imperishable (Brahman); but (there is) inclusion (of all these negations everywhere); because of sameness (of method) and its being-that-Brahman; like the Upasads; that is stated
3.3.34
इयदामननात्
because both mantras teach the object as just-this-much (twofold)
3.3.35
अन्तरा भूतग्रामवत्स्वात्मनः
in between, in the interval (of the two recitations); like the aggregate of elements; of one's own (innermost) self
3.3.36
अन्यथा भेदानुपपत्तिरिति चेन्नोपदेशान्तरवत्
otherwise (if difference of vidyā not admitted); non-establishment of the (textual) difference — thus (objection); if so — no; as in another instance of teaching
3.3.37
व्यतिहारो विशिंषन्ति हीतरवत्
the reciprocity (mutual interchange); enjoined for meditation; they (the reciters) specify, by uttering both forms; for, like the other (qualities enjoined for meditation)
3.3.38
सैव हि सत्यादयः
that very (Satya-vidyā) itself; for, because (gives the reason); satya and the rest (the worshipped elements)
3.3.39
कामादीतरत्र तत्र चायतनादिभ्यः
the satyakāma-etc. qualities (belong) in the other text; there (likewise in each recension); and because of common abode (heart) etc.
3.3.40
आदरादलोपः
No elision [of prāṇāgnihotra], because of [the śruti's] regard
3.3.41
उपस्थितेऽतस्तद्वचनात्
When food is present, from this (food-substance), because scripture states it
3.3.42
तन्निर्धारणानियमस्तद्दृष्टेः पृथग्घ्यप्रतिबन्धः फलम्
No fixed rule for those determinations, since that is (so) seen; for indeed (their) fruit, separately, is unobstructed; the fruit (result)
3.3.43
प्रदानवदेव तदुक्तम्
just as in the offering (case), indeed; that has (already) been stated
3.3.44
