4.1.1
आवृत्तिरसकृदुपदेशात्
Repetition (of the cognition) is enjoined, because of the more-than-once teaching
4.1.2
लिङ्गाच्च
and from the indicatory mark (liṅga)
4.1.3
आत्मेति तूपगच्छन्ति ग्राहयन्ति च
"as the Self" — the Lord is to be understood as the Self; but (the teachers) accept/approach (Him thus); and make (others) grasp (Him as the Self); and
4.1.4
न प्रतीके न हि सः
not; on the symbol (of meditation); not; for, because; he (the worshipper)
4.1.5
ब्रह्मदृष्टिरुत्कर्षात्
The Brahman-view (is superimposed on the symbol), because of exaltation
4.1.6
आदित्यादिमतयश्चाङ्ग उपपत्तेः
And the (udgītha-etc.) conceptions [are superimposed] on the sacrificial member; because of consistency (reasonableness, logical fitness)
4.1.7
आसीनः सम्भवात्
seated; (one who is) sitting; because (it is) possible; from feasibility
4.1.8
ध्यानाच्च
And because of (the verb implying) meditation
4.1.9
अचलत्वं चापेक्ष्य
immovability, motionlessness (the object regarded); and having regard to / with reference to
4.1.10
स्मरन्ति च
they (the wise) remember, i.e. teach in smṛti; and, also
4.1.11
यत्रैकाग्रता तत्राविशेषात्
where one-pointedness of mind arises easily; there meditate, since no place-distinction is enjoined
4.1.12
आ प्रायणात्तत्रापि हि दृष्टम्
up to, until (governing the ablative); until death; there too it is (so); for, because; (it is) seen, observed (in śruti)
4.1.13
तदधिगम उत्तरपूर्वाघयोरश्लेषविनाशौ तद्व्यपदेशात्
On attaining that (Brahman); when Brahman is known; Non-clinging (of later) and destruction (of earlier) sins; Because scripture so declares (that effect)
4.1.14
इतरस्याप्येवमसंश्लेषः पाते तु
Of the other (merit) too, likewise non-contact; at the fall (of the body), i.e. death; but (marks the distinction)
4.1.15
अनारब्धकार्ये एव तु पूर्वे तदवधेः
only the two deeds whose effects have not begun; only, alone (emphatic restriction); but (marking the established conclusion); the prior (accumulated) two deeds; because release has that (body-fall) as its limit
4.1.16
अग्निहोत्रादि तु तत्कार्यायैव तद्दर्शनात्
The Agnihotra and other obligatory Vedic rites; but (dispels the doubt); for the very effect of that (knowledge) alone; because that is seen (in scripture)
4.1.17
अतोऽन्यापि ह्येकेषामुभयोः
hence too there is another (good deed); for, of some (recensions); of both teachers (Jaimini, Bādarāyaṇa)
4.1.18
यदेव विद्ययेति हि
that very (act) which (one does) — emphatic; 'with knowledge' — thus (closing the quoted Śruti); for, because (adducing the Śruti as reason)
4.1.19
भोगेन त्वितरे क्षपयित्वा सम्पद्यते
through enjoyment (of prārabdha merit and demerit); but the other two (prārabdha merit and demerit); having exhausted (them) by enjoyment; (the knower) attains Brahman, becomes Brahman
4.2.1
वाङ्मनसि दर्शनाच्छब्दाच्च
the function of speech (merges) into the mind; because it is observed and because of scripture
4.2.2
अत एव च सर्वाण्यनु
hence, for this very reason; very, precisely (emphatic); and; all (the sense-organs) follow (the mind)
4.2.3
तन्मनः प्राण उत्तरात्
that mind; (merges) into the vital breath; from the latter statement
4.2.4
सोऽध्यक्षे तदुपगमादिभ्यः
that (prāṇa) abides in the overseer (vijñānātman); because of (prāṇas') approaching that overseer, and such reasons
4.2.5
भूतेषु तच्छ्रुतेः
in the (subtle, tejas-accompanied) elements; because of the śruti to that effect
4.2.6
नैकस्मिन्दर्शयतो हि
Not in one (tejas) alone; the two (śruti-smṛti) show this; for; indeed (giving the reason)
4.2.7
समाना चासृत्युपक्रमादमृतत्वं चानुपोष्य
identical (departure same for knower and non-knower); and up to the start of the path; and immortality; and without having burnt away (the body/bonds)
4.2.8
तदाऽपीतेः संसारव्यपदेशात्
That (subtle element) abides until dissolution/liberation from saṃsāra; because of the scriptural designation of transmigration
4.2.9
सूक्ष्मं प्रमाणतश्च तथोपलब्धेः
subtle (qualifying tejas), in nature and in measure; and in respect of measure/dimension; because it is so perceived (apprehended thus)
4.2.10
नोपमर्देनातः
Not (destroyed) by (gross body's) destruction; hence, due to subtlety
4.2.11
अस्यैव चोपपत्तेरेष ऊष्मा
of this very (subtle body); and, because it is fitting (so), this; heat (belonging to the subtle body)
4.2.12
प्रतिषेधादिति चेन्न शारीरात्
because of the prohibition (of departure) — thus (objects the opponent); if (so objected) — no (it is refuted); from the embodied self (jīva), not from the body
4.2.13
स्पष्टो ह्येकेषाम्
clear, manifest (the prohibition is plainly perceived); for, of some (reciters/recensions)
4.2.14
स्मर्यते च
it is also recorded in smṛti (Mahābhārata); and (likewise)
4.2.15
तानि परे तथा ह्याह
those (the senses and elements); in the supreme (Self); thus, so; for so (the śruti) says
4.2.16
अविभागो वचनात्
non-separation; merging without residue into the supreme; because of the (scriptural) statement
4.2.17
तदोकोऽग्रज्वलनं तत्प्रकाशितद्वारो विद्यासामर्थ्यात्तच्छेषगत्यनुस्मृतियोगाच्च हार्दानुगृहीतः शताधिकया
the blazing of the tip of its abode, the heart; having the egress-doors illumined by that (blazing tip); from power of knowledge and union with recollecting its onward course; favoured by the one seated in the heart (the Lord); by the hundred-and-first nāḍī (suṣumnā), exceeding the hundred
4.2.18
रश्म्यनुसारी
ray-following (departing soul follows the sun's rays)
4.2.19
निशि नेति चेन्न सम्बन्धस्य यावद्देहभावित्वाद्दर्शयति च
at night; "not (so)" — thus (the objector says); if (so urged), no; of the (nāḍī-ray) connection; because it lasts as long as the body; (śruti) shows; and (also)
4.2.20
अतश्चायनेऽपि दक्षिणे
And therefore even when (one dies) in the (solstitial) course; in the southern (course), i.e. dakṣiṇāyana
4.2.21
योगिनः प्रति च स्मर्यते स्मार्ते चैते
the yogins (object of प्रति: toward the yogins); toward, with regard to (the yogins); and; is remembered, is taught in smṛti; the two are smārta (smṛti-based): Yoga and Sāṅkhya; and these two (the Yoga and Sāṅkhya paths)
4.3.1
अर्चिरादिना तत्प्रथितेः
by the path beginning with the flame (of the gods); because that (path) is well-known (in the scriptures)
4.3.2
वायुमब्दादविशेषविशेषाभ्याम्
(soul reaches) Vāyu after the year, by non-specification and specification
4.3.3
तडितोऽधि वरुणः सम्बन्धात्
above the lightning; Varuṇa (is connected above lightning); because of their connection
4.3.4
आतिवाहिकास्तल्लिङ्गात्
(They are) conveyers; because of a mark of that
4.3.5
उभयव्यामोहात्तत्सिद्धेः
From the perplexity of both (soul, path-marks), that (conducting deities) is established
4.3.6
वैद्युतेनैव ततस्तच्छ्रुतेः
by the very lightning-(person), the non-human conveyer; thence, because śruti states his (lightning-person's) conveying
4.3.7
कार्यं बादरिरस्य गत्युपपत्तेः
the effected (saguṇa, lower) Brahman; Bādari (the teacher holds); of this (kārya-Brahman); because the going (to it) is logically possible
4.3.8
विशेषितत्वाच्च
and because of the specification (by the plural)
4.3.9
सामीप्यात्तु तद्व्यपदेशः
Because of proximity (to Para-Brahman); but/however; that designation (the word Brahman applied to it)
4.3.10
कार्यात्यये तदध्यक्षेण सहातः परमभिधानात्
when the effected (Brahmā's) world dissolves; together with its ruler (Hiraṇyagarbha); together with [him]; hence (beyond this); the supreme (place); because of scriptural declaration
4.3.11
स्मृतेश्च
and from smṛti (which also acknowledges this)
4.3.12
परं जैमिनिर्मुख्यत्वात्
the highest (Brahman), as object; Jaimini (holds); because of the primary sense
4.3.13
दर्शनाच्च
And because (śruti) indicates (the going to Brahman)
4.3.14
न च कार्ये प्रतिपत्त्यभिसन्धिः
not; and; with regard to the effected [created Brahman]; resolve to attain (reach the abode)
4.3.15
अप्रतीकालम्बनान्नयतीति बादरायण उभयथाऽदोषात्तत्क्रतुश्च
He leads those whose support is not a symbol, so [Bādarāyaṇa] says; Bādarāyaṇa (the teacher who holds this view); Because no fault arises either way; and from the tatkratu principle
4.3.16
विशेषं च दर्शयति
a distinction (in fruit, i.e. फलविशेष); and; also; (the śruti) shows, points out
4.4.1
सम्पद्याविर्भावः स्वेनशब्दात्
On attaining (release), there is manifestation in its own form; because of the word 'svena' (in 'svena rūpeṇa' śruti)
4.4.2
मुक्तः प्रतिज्ञानात्
liberated (the released soul, free from all bondage); because of the (scriptural) declaration/assertion
4.4.3
आत्मा प्रकरणात्
the Self (is meant by 'jyotis'); because of the context (the Self being the topic)
4.4.4
अविभागेन दृष्टत्वात्
non-separately; in an undivided manner (from the supreme Self); because it is (so) seen, i.e. scripturally taught
4.4.5
ब्राह्मेण जैमिनिरुपन्यासादिभ्यः
by the Brahman-nature (the self's own true form); Jaimini [holds]; from the scriptural expositions and the like
4.4.6
चितितन्मात्रेण तदात्मकत्वादित्यौडुलोमिः
by mere consciousness alone (the self's sole essence); because the self has consciousness as its nature—thus Auḍulomi holds
4.4.7
एवमप्युपन्यासात्पूर्वभावादविरोधं बादरायणः
even so, no contradiction — since the prior [lordliness], known from the statements, persists; Bādarāyaṇa, the teacher, holds [there is no contradiction]
4.4.8
सङ्कल्पादेव तु तच्छ्रुतेः
from mere will alone (ablative of cause); but (introduces the established conclusion); because scripture (śruti) states that
4.4.9
अत एव चानन्याधिपतिः
from this, for this very reason; indeed, precisely (emphatic); and (the knower has) no other overlord
4.4.10
अभावं बादरिराह ह्येवम्
absence, non-existence (of body and senses in release); the teacher Bādari says/holds; for thus (the scripture declares)
4.4.11
भावं जैमिनिर्विकल्पामननात्
existence (of body+senses for the liberated); object of मन्यते; Jaimini (holds); because scripture teaches the manifoldness
4.4.12
द्वादशाहवदुभयविधं बादरायणोऽतः
of both kinds, like the twelve-day sacrifice; Bādarāyaṇa (holds this), for this very reason
4.4.13
तन्वभावे सन्ध्यवदुपपत्तेः
in the absence of the body (with its organs); because it is fitting, as in the intermediate (dream) state
4.4.14
भावे जाग्रद्वत्
on the manifestation (of a body, tanu); just as in the waking state
4.4.15
प्रदीपवदावेशस्तथा हि दर्शयति
the entering (of bodies) is lamp-like; thus; for, because (gives the reason); (scripture) shows, declares
4.4.16
स्वाप्ययसम्पत्त्योरन्यतरापेक्षमाविष्कृतं हि
revealed regarding one of the two states: deep-sleep or isolation; for; because
4.4.17
जगद्व्यापारवर्जं प्रकरणादसन्निहितत्वाच्च
excepting the world's operation (creation etc.); from the context, and from (others') non-proximity (to it)
4.4.18
प्रत्यक्षोपदेशादिति चेन्नाधिकारिकमण्डलस्थोक्तेः
"because of direct (scriptural) teaching" — thus (the objection); if so — no; because of statement about presiding Lord situated in the orb
4.4.19
विकारावर्ति च तथा हि स्थितिमाह
not revolving in modification; transcending modification (the supreme form); and; also; thus, so (for indeed); for, because, indeed; (scripture) declares (its) twofold existence/state
4.4.20
दर्शयतश्चैवं प्रत्यक्षानुमाने
and thus the two (śruti and smṛti) show; perception and inference (i.e. śruti and smṛti)
4.4.21
भोगमात्रसाम्यलिङ्गाच्च
and because of the mark of equality merely in enjoyment
4.4.22
अनावृत्तिः शब्दादनावृत्तिः शब्दात्
non-return (of the liberated); no rebirth; because of scripture; non-return; because of (Vedic) scripture/word
