About the book
A compendium of Advaita Vedānta in 1006 verses: the four qualifications of the seeker, superimposition and the creation of the world, the sheaths, the Self as existence, consciousness and bliss, the meaning of tat tvam asi, samādhi, and liberation while living and after death. Much of it is a dialogue between a teacher and his disciple.
