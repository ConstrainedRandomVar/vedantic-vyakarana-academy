6 · द्वितीय आक्षेपः
शिष्यःननु हरिहरादयः देवाः मायाविशिष्टचैतन्यात्मकेश्वररूपसमुद्रस्य तरङ्गाः इति वक्तुम् युक्त्तम्, न तु तव ।
Now, it may be argued: It is appropriate to say that deities like Hari and Hara are waves in the ocean of Īśvara, whose nature is Consciousness qualified by māyā, but not according to your view.
शिष्यःअतः ईश्वरविषयकम् मङ्गलाचरणम् युज्यते ।
Therefore, offering an auspicious invocation addressed to Īśvara is justified.
शिष्यःकिञ्च वृक्षमूलसेचनेन यथा तच्छाखास्कन्धादयः तृप्यन्ति, यथा अन्नपानादिजनितप्राणतृप्त्या इन्द्रियादितृप्तिः, एवम् ईश्वरविषयकमङ्गलाचरणेन एव तद्विभूतिभूतेतरदेवतामङ्गलाचरणम् सिद्ध्येत्, न तु त्वन्मङ्गलाचरणात् इति चेत् ।
Furthermore, just as watering the root of a tree satisfies its branches and trunk, or as satisfying the vital breath through food and drink satisfies the senses, so too the auspicious invocation of other deities who are His manifestations would be accomplished solely by an auspicious invocation addressed to Īśvara, but not by your invocation — if this objection is raised:
गुरुःअस्य ग्रन्थस्य जीवब्रह्मैक्यम् विषयः इत्यनेन मङ्गलेन सूच्यते ।
By this auspicious invocation, it is indicated that the subject matter of this text is the identity of jīva and Brahman.
116 · गुरुसेवाक्रमः
श्रीसद्गुरुलाभोत्तरक्षणे तद्दर्शनमात्रात् साष्टाङ्गं 1नमस्कृत्य तच्चरणकमलरजांसि परिशुद्धतमानि स्वोत्तमाङ्गे धारयेत् ।
In the immediate moment of obtaining the revered true Guru, merely upon seeing him, one should prostrate with eight limbs and place the most supremely pure dust of his lotus feet upon one's own head.
पश्चात् सद्गुरुप्रसादाद्बन्धमोचनाकाङ्क्षी तीव्रतरमुमुक्षुर्गुरुकुले ब्रह्मचारी वसेत् ।
Thereafter, desiring release from bondage through the grace of the true Guru, an intensely earnest mumukṣu should reside as a celibate student in the Gurukula.
तस्मै स्वशरीरमनोवाग्धनानि समर्पयेत् ।
One should surrender one's own body, mind, speech, and wealth to him.
टिप्पणी

पद्भ्यां कराभ्यामूरुभ्यामुरसा शिरसा भुवि । पतनं दीर्घतरुवत् प्रणामोऽष्टाङ्ग उच्यते ॥
'Falling flat on the ground like a long tree using the two feet, two hands, two thighs, chest, and head is called prostration with eight limbs.'
पद्भ्यां कराभ्यां जानुभ्यामुरसा शिरसा दृशा । वचसा मनसा चैव प्रणामोऽष्टाङ्ग उच्यते ॥
'Prostration performed with two feet, two hands, two knees, chest, head, eyes, speech, and mind is called eight-limbed prostration.'

मृगयार्थं वनं गतो राजा भर्च्छुं दृष्ट्वा भीतः पलायते
The king, having gone to the forest for hunting, sees Bharcchu, becomes frightened, and flees.
भर्च्छुस्थानापन्नो मन्त्री भर्च्छुवृत्तान्तं सर्वं श्रुत्वा स्वानुयायिभिः सहैवं समालोचयत् - यदि राजा भर्च्छुर्जीवति इति लोकवार्तां श‍ृणोति, अथवा मृगयार्थं वनं गत्वा स्वयमेव भर्च्छुं पश्यति तदा मिथ्यावादिनोऽस्मान् स राजा सुतीक्ष्णं दण्डयेत् इति ।
The minister who had occupied Bharcchu's position, hearing the whole story of Bharcchu, deliberated with his followers thus: 'If the king hears the rumor that Bharcchu is alive, or if he goes to the forest for hunting and sees Bharcchu himself, then that king will severely punish us as liars.'
एवमालोच्य सपरिवारो राजानमासाद्य दस्युभिर्युद्धे मृतो भर्च्छुः पिशाचो भूत्वा भस्मोद्धूलितसर्वाङ्गो वने सञ्चरति । स्वदृष्टिगोचरापन्नं सर्वं जनं मारयति । यस्तं दृष्ट्वा सुदूरं पलायते स जीवेत् इति विज्ञापयामास ।
Having deliberated thus, along with his retinue, he approached the king and informed him: 'Bharcchu, killed in battle by robbers, has become a demon and roams the forest with his entire body smeared in ashes. He kills everyone who comes within sight. Only he who sees him and flees far away may survive.'
मन्त्रिवाक्यश्रवणमात्रेण राजा तत्त्वमविचारयन्, मृतो भर्च्छुः पिशाचः समभवत् इति निश्चिकाय ।
Merely upon hearing the minister's words, the king, without investigating the truth, concluded that 'The deceased Bharcchu has become a demon'.
ततः कदाचिदाखेटार्थं गतो भर्च्छुवनं प्रविवेश ।
Then, having gone hunting on a certain day, he entered Bharcchu's forest.
तत्र तरुमूले तपश्चरन्तं भर्च्छुं दृष्ट्वा प्रतारकमन्त्रिवाक्यं स्मरन् नूनमयं भर्च्छुः पिशाचः इति भयेन पलायितः ।
There, seeing Bharcchu performing penance at the root of a tree and remembering the deceitful minister's words, thinking 'Surely this Bharcchu is a demon!', he fled in fear.
अयमर्थः - अर्थस्य सम्पादने वर्धने चातिदुःखम् ।
This is the point: There is extreme sorrow in acquiring and multiplying wealth.
रक्षणे महद्दुःखम् ।
There is great sorrow in protecting it.
व्यये भोगे च महती चिन्ता ।
There is great anxiety in spending and enjoying it.
नाशे चित्तभ्रान्तिः ।
Upon its loss, there is mental delusion.
अर्थप्राप्तये स्तेयादयः षडनर्थाः ।
For acquiring wealth, there are six evils such as theft.
प्राप्तेऽर्थे स्मयादयः षडनर्थास्त्रिविधव्यसनैः सह नवानर्थाः ।
Once wealth is obtained, there are six evils such as arrogance, which together with the three types of vices make nine evils.
तस्मादेकोऽप्यर्थः पञ्चदशानर्थानां निदानं भवति ।
Therefore, even a single piece of wealth becomes the primary cause of fifteen evils.
व्यसनानि = स्त्रीद्यूतमद्यविषयाणि त्रीणि ।
Vices = three, namely women, gambling, and wine.
एकास्रिग्धाः = एके = एकप्राणाः, ते च ते आस्रिग्धाश्चातिप्रिया इत्यर्थः ।
Ekāsrigdhāḥ = eke = sharing the same life-breath, and those who are attached and exceedingly beloved, this is the meaning.

'Kākaṇiḥ' is an archaic masculine usage.

(Kākiṇī) kākaṇiḥ = twenty cowrie shells.
